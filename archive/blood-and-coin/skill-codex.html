    Your skills

    Blood & Coin borrows its character building from Path of Exile 2. Every class shares one enormous passive tree, skills are gems you cut yourself, support gems bend those skills into something new, and an ascendancy turns your class into a specialist.

    The passive tree

    One tree of roughly 3,800 passives for all twelve classes. Each class starts on its own spot: Strength classes bottom-left, Dexterity right, Intelligence at the top, hybrids in between.

    • You gain 1 passive point per level (139 by level 140) plus up to 24 from quest books.
    • You can only take a passive next to one you already have: your tree grows outward.
    • Refunding costs gold and only works if the rest of your tree stays connected.

    Clusters & notables

    Named notables sit in the middle of little wheels of small passives, just like PoE 2's clusters. Walk into the wheel through its entry, collect the small bonuses, and take the notable at the back.

    Keystones

    24 keystones sit on the outer rim. Each one changes a rule — split your spell damage across fire, cold and lightning, pay skills with health instead of stamina — always with a real drawback. They are a long walk away on purpose.

    Attributes

    Attribute passives let you choose +5 Strength, Dexterity or Intelligence. Strength gives 2 health per point, Dexterity 2 evasion, Intelligence 1 stamina. Gems need your main attribute: a level 20 gem needs 62 of it.

    Ascendancy

    Every class has two ascendancies with opposite play styles. Complete the four Trials of Ascension in the mega-dungeons beneath Kingsbridge, Greyhold, Northwatch and Blackcrown for 2 points each (8 in total). Each branch is a small node, then its notable. Capstones need a notable from an earlier branch.

    Skill gems

    Gems drop uncut with a level. When you use one, you pick which skill to cut from it — any skill up to that level, from any class. A higher uncut gem levels up a skill you already own (1–20). Higher gem levels hit harder but need a higher character level and more of the gem's attribute.

    Support gems

    Every skill has 2 support sockets. Jeweller's Orbs raise that to 3 (Lesser), 4 (Greater) and 5 (Perfect). Supports only fit skills with matching tags — Scattershot needs a projectile, Horde needs minions. A skill can hold each support only once, but you can use the same support on different skills. Removing a support destroys it.

    Spirit

    Persistent skills — auras, banners, permanent skeletons — have no cooldown. They stay on and reserve Spirit instead. You start with 30 Spirit; passives and gear add more. Supports can raise or lower how much a persistent skill reserves.

    Weapon sets

    From level 6 you earn weapon-set points (up to 24). Passives bought with them only count while you hold weapon set I or II, so a bow build can carry a melee backup.

    Controls in the game

    P opens this screen, 1–4 R T use skills, X swaps weapon sets. On an AZERTY keyboard you walk with ZQSD, on QWERTY with WASD — change it in Settings.
